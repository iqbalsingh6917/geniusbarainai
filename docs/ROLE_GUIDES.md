## Role Guides (Quick Reference)

### SUPERADMIN
- **Dashboards:** `/superadmin/dashboard` and analytics cards.
- **Curriculum:** Manage courses/modules/levels/worksheets/exams via Abacus builder.
- **Licensing/Finance:** Full visibility and adjustments.
- **Leads/Sales:** Access all leads and stages.

### BUSINESS_PARTNER
- **Dashboard:** `/business-partner/dashboard` with org-wide metrics and lead funnel.
- **Leads:** `/business-partner/leads` (create, update, stage changes within your org tree).
- **Reports/Finance:** Scoped to your subtree.

### FRANCHISE
- **Dashboard:** `/franchise/dashboard`.
- **Leads:** `/franchise/leads` (scoped to franchise + centers).
- **Org/Finance:** Scoped summaries for your franchise.

### CENTER_MANAGER
- **Dashboard:** `/center/dashboard`.
- **Leads:** `/center/leads` (center-only).
- **Attendance/Enrollments:** Center-scoped visibility.

### TEACHER
- **Assessments:** Review worksheets/exams at `/teacher/worksheets/review/...` and `/teacher/exams/attempts`.
- **Analytics:** Scoped assessment summaries on teacher dashboard.

### STUDENT
- **Worksheets:** `/student/worksheets/:worksheetId/play` or `/student/worksheet-attempts/:attemptId/play`.
- **Exams:** `/student/exams/:examId/play` or `/student/exam-attempts/:attemptId/play`.
- **Status:** See scores after submission; read-only once submitted.
