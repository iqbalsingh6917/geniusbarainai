# Center Role Feature Checklist

1) Dashboard
   - Center-level dashboard backend endpoints exist: ⚠️
   - Dashboard UI page exists: ✔️
   - Quick actions / navigation for center: ⚠️
   - Summary of teachers/students/enrollments under center: ✔️

2) Org Management
   - View teachers under center: ✔️
   - Add/edit teacher users (if permitted): ❌
   - View coordinator roles (if applicable): ❌

3) Students & Enrollments
   - View students assigned to the center: ✔️
   - Add/edit student details: ⚠️
   - View enrollments: ✔️
   - Create/manage enrollments: ⚠️

4) Licensing (Center Scope)
   - View seats allocated to the center by franchise/BP: ❌
   - Visibility of remaining seats (if applicable): ❌
   - No seat allocation ability: ✔️

5) Curriculum Access
   - View curriculum mapped to center courses: ✔️
   - Zero editing permissions: ✔️

6) Teacher Assignments
   - Assign teachers to students or classes: ✔️
   - Modify class schedules (if applicable): ⚠️

7) Leads / Sales (Center Level)
   - Access to leads (if center inherits sales module): ❌
   - Ability to edit leads: ❌
   - Funnel/analytics visibility: ❌

8) Reports & Performance
   - View student progress reports: ⚠️
   - View teacher performance metrics: ❌
   - View attendance/learning reports: ✔️
